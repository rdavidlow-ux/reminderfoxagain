# ReminderFoxAgain — the English strings to translate (the keys of src/ui/locales/<code>.json).
# One key per line; "a || b" gives two keys the same translation. {n}, {n2}… are numbers; other {names} are carried over
# as they are (a date, a time, a name, or text that is translated on its own). A translation may give plural forms:
#   one=… | few=… | many=… | other=…   (the categories of Intl.PluralRules for the language)
# tools/i18n/build.js turns tools/i18n/<code>.txt (one translation per line, in this order) into src/ui/locales/<code>.json.
