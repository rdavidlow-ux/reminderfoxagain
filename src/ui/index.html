About

ReminderFoxAgain is a tribute to the original ReminderFox add-on for Firefox and Thunderbird, created by Tom Mutdosch and Daniel Lee with Guenter Wahl. Its file format, repeat rules, views, quick alarms and alert slider are theirs; thank you.
